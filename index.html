Allergen & ingredient notice

Shared production. All Sottozero gelato is produced in our gelato laboratory using shared equipment, and every flavour is stored and served side by side in our shops. Each flavour has its own scoop and we follow strict hygiene procedures, but traces of any allergen may be present in any product. We cannot guarantee that any product is free from any allergen.

Contains and may contain. “Contains” means the allergen is an ingredient. “May contain” means it is not an ingredient, but traces may be present. Every product may contain traces of gluten, eggs, milk, nuts, fish and crustaceans.

The choice is yours. This page is for guidance only. It is based on our recipes and our suppliers’ ingredient information, which can change without notice. We cannot assess your allergy or decide for you whether a product is suitable. Always tell our team about any allergy before you order. If you have a severe allergy, we advise you not to eat our products; if you choose to, you do so at your own risk. If in doubt, do not consume.
